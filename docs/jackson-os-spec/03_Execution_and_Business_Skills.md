# Jackson OS | Execution and Business Skills
Version 2.0 — 2026-11-25 | Proposed implementation specification

# Opportunity engine
Collect permitted trend, search, marketplace and competitor signals. Normalize sources, deduplicate, timestamp and score demand, saturation, margin, shipping, returns, IP risk and evidence. Produce ranked opportunity briefs with source links and uncertainty. TikTok and other social data require authorized access; do not promise unrestricted scraping.
# E-commerce pipeline
Research products -> validate supplier and landed cost -> draft offer and brand -> create Shopify development store or draft products -> generate product copy and media -> test checkout and fulfillment -> approval -> publish -> monitor conversion, returns and margin. Shopify Admin API access and payment setup require owner authorization. Never invent reviews or product claims.
# Virtual influencer pipeline
Create original fictional persona, brand bible, consent-cleared visual assets, voice and content calendar. Generate scripts, images, short videos, captions and edits; review likeness, disclosure, rights and platform rules; schedule via approved APIs; measure engagement and conversions. No impersonation or fake engagement.
# Website and SaaS factory
Translate opportunity into requirements, wireframes, repo, implementation, database migrations, tests, accessibility checks, preview deployment and approval. Use reusable templates and versioned components. Verify domain ownership, payments, privacy notices and rollback before launch.
# Agency and sales
Find lawful business leads, enrich with permitted sources, deduplicate, score, draft personalized outreach, respect opt-outs and applicable calling/email rules, book consultations, generate proposals and track CRM stages. Humanize copy for clarity and brand voice, not deception or evasion. Fulfill client projects in isolated tenant workspaces.
# Other departments
Marketing: SEO, campaign drafts, content repurposing, clipping, analytics and experiments. Finance: cost ledger, cash-flow forecasts and profitability. Operations: support triage, inventory alerts, documentation. Trading: backtests and paper trading; real estate: comparable research and underwriting. Legal/admin: draft and route for qualified review.
